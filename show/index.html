Hidden keypad
On the fake Lock Screen, the area under the clock is an invisible phone keypad: 1 2 3 / 4 5 6 / 7 8 9 / * 0 #. Type a value and tap # to send it to the next slot below; the torch button glows briefly when it's sent. * = a space between numbers, * * = start the entry again. Hold anywhere for a second to peek (values, what you've typed); let go to hide it. Double tap the clock = reveal the newspaper; hold the clock 2 s = back to your picture; hold the torch 2 s = back here.
